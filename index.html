Bonus Feature

Take a Real IQ Test

Modeled after official standardized tests like WAIS and Raven's Progressive Matrices. 90 questions across 5 cognitive domains.

🧠

5 Domains

Pattern, Numbers, Verbal, Spatial, Logic

▼

Each domain tests a different aspect of cognitive ability: Pattern Recognition, Number Sequences, Verbal Reasoning, Spatial Reasoning, and Logical Reasoning. Your score is broken down by domain so you can see your strengths.

📊

Real Scoring

Mean 100, SD 15, percentile ranks

▼

Uses the standard normal distribution (mean 100, standard deviation 15) just like official IQ tests. Get your percentile rank and IQ classification (Average, Above Average, Superior, etc.).

🔄

30 Per Test

Randomly selected from 90 questions

▼

Each test randomly selects 30 questions from a pool of 90, so every retake feels completely different. No memorizing answers — it's a fresh challenge every time.

⏱

No Time Limit

Take as long as you need

▼

There's no countdown timer pressuring you. Take as long as you need on each question. Time is tracked and factored into your final score, but you won't be cut off.
